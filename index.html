About InsurMatch

InsurMatch is a digital technology and lead-generation platform. We do not sell insurance policies, underwrite plans, or collect premiums. Instead, we match consumers with independent, state-licensed insurance professionals who provide personalized guidance and plan options.

How It Works

  1. Tell Us What You Need: Select your insurance category (Medicare, ACA Health, Life) and location.
  2. Information Verification: We verify your details to match you with an agent licensed in your state.
  3. Intelligent Matching: Our platform pairs you with qualified bilingual independent agents.
  4. Connect & Review: A licensed agent contacts you with customized options at zero obligation.

Coverage Categories Supported

  • Medicare Guidance: Medicare Advantage (Part C), Prescription Drug (Part D), and Medigap Supplements.
  • Affordable Care Act (ACA): Marketplace health insurance plans and federal premium subsidy eligibility.
  • Life & Asset Protection: Term life, permanent policies with living benefits, and fixed indexed annuities.
